About the company

ARS Traffic & Transport Technology (ARS T&TT), headquartered in the Netherlands has been providing Intelligent traffic and transport technology solutions to businesses and government bodies for over 24 years. We are an active player in our home market of the Netherlands, with special focus on the international emerging traffic and transport technology needs.

Our mission as an independent ‘Intelligent Transport System’ (ITS) solutions provider is to rapidly expand the leading position in the fast-growing smart traffic and transportation markets. The unwavering focus on continuous innovation enables us to achieve this mission.
